The Best Movies According to Clarence

My top three(3) movies of all-time.

Bourne Supremacy

The Bourne Supremacy follows Jason Bourne, a former CIA assassin, who is framed for a botched CIA operation. As he tries to clear his name, he is drawn back into a dangerous world of espionage, leading to a tragic event that propels him on a quest for revenge and the truth about his past.

Law Abiding Citizen

Law Abiding Citizen follows Clyde Shelton, a man whose wife and daughter are brutally murdered during a home invasion. After the justice system fails to adequately punish the killers, Clyde takes matters into his own hands, seeking vengeance against those involved in the case, including the prosecutor who made a plea deal with one of the murderers.

Radio

In "Radio," a high school football coach named Harold Jones befriends a mentally disabled man known as Radio, who becomes a beloved figure in their small town. As Radio assists the team and gains confidence, he faces challenges from those who see him as a distraction, but ultimately, he inspires the community with his kindness and spirit.
